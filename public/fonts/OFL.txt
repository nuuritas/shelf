The web fonts in this directory are subsets of three SIL Open Font License 1.1
typefaces. They are served from this origin; neither the site nor the app loads
a font from a third-party host. The same three families ship as static TTFs to
the native clients (`apps/ios/Shelf/Fonts`, `apps/android/assets/fonts`), so
every surface shows the same letters.

Figtree
  Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors
  https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree
  Instanced at weights 400, 500 and 600.

Onest
  Copyright 2021 The Onest Project Authors
  https://github.com/googlefonts/onest
  Instanced at weights 400, 500 and 600.

JetBrains Mono
  Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors
  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono
  Instanced at weights 400 and 500.

All three were reduced to the Latin and Latin Extended character sets (which
covers Turkish) with fonttools:

  fonttools varLib.instancer <variable>.woff2 wght=500 -o <name>.woff2
  pyftsubset <name>.woff2 --flavor=woff2 --unicodes=... --output-file=<subset>

Files: figtree-{400,500,600}-{latin,latin-ext},
onest-{400,500,600}-{latin,latin-ext},
jetbrains-mono-{400,500}-{latin,latin-ext}.

Figtree is the display face (wordmark, headlines, card titles). Onest is the
interface face (body, labels, buttons, navigation). JetBrains Mono is only for
values: refs, codes, counts, sizes and addresses. No italic is used anywhere.

The full license text for each family is in OFL-Figtree.txt, OFL-Onest.txt and
OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt in this directory.
